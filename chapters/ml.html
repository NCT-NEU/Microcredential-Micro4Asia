Applied machine learning in economics and business#

MP Title#

Applied machine learning in economics and business

  • Class contact: 15 hours

  • Independent study: 30 hours

MP Aim#

Develop practical competence in applying machine learning models to economic and business problems using Python, with emphasis on model construction, evaluation, interpretation, and decision support in real-world contexts.

Topic No.

Teaching topic

Content description

Foundations of applied machine learning in economics and business

Role of machine learning in finance, marketing, supply chain and risk management.
Overview of supervised learning models.
Python ML ecosystem including scikit-learn, numpy, pandas and matplotlib.

Regression models for economic forecasting

Linear regression, ridge regression and lasso regression.
Evaluation using RMSE and R² metrics.

Classification models for decision making

Logistic regression, k-nearest neighbors and support vector machines.
Applications in credit scoring and customer segmentation.
Accuracy, precision, recall and confusion matrix.

Tree-based and ensemble models

Decision trees, random forest and gradient boosting models.
Model robustness and feature importance analysis.
Business use cases in churn prediction and fraud detection.

LO.2–LO.4

5

Model evaluation and selection strategies

Cross-validation and model tuning concepts.
Bias–variance tradeoff explanation.
Selection criteria for business decision-making contexts.

LO.4

Model interpretation and explainability

Interpretation of coefficients and tree structures.
Feature importance and explainability concepts.
Linking model predictions to strategic business decisions.

End-to-end application of multiple models on an economic or business dataset.
Comparison of model performance and decision implications.

Teaching and learning methods#

Teaching Method

Description

Related learning outcomes

Conceptual lectures and model demonstrations

Introduces machine learning concepts and algorithms in applied economic and business contexts.

Guided hands-on coding practice

Develops practical ability to implement ML models using scikit-learn, pandas and numpy.

Enables learners to compare multiple models and identify optimal solutions.

Assessment methods#

Assessment Type

Description

Purpose

Project-based assignment: applied ML case study

Learners develop and compare multiple machine learning models for a real economic or business dataset and provide actionable recommendations.

Assesses integrated ability to apply, evaluate and interpret ML models.

Scenario-based quiz

Multiple-choice and short-answer questions on model selection, performance metrics and algorithm interpretation.

Tests conceptual understanding of applied ML principles.

Pre-requisites#

  • Mathematical foundations for data science

  • Python programming for data science

  • Data cleaning and preprocessing with Python

Libraries and tools used#

  • scikit-learn – machine learning algorithms

  • pandas – structured data handling

  • numpy – numerical computation

  • matplotlib – basic result visualization

  • joblib – model saving and deployment support

Literature#

  • Géron, A. (2022). Hands-on machine learning with scikit-learn, keras, and tensorflow (3rd ed.). O’Reilly Media.

  • Kuhn, M., & Johnson, K. (2013). Applied predictive modeling. Springer.

  • Shmueli, G., Bruce, P., & Gedeck, P. (2020). Data mining for business analytics. Wiley.

Comments#

This micro-credential emphasizes the practical deployment of machine learning techniques in economics and business applications. The course focuses on interpretable and applicable models that support informed decision-making, strategic planning, and performance optimization, while deliberately excluding deep learning to maintain clarity, relevance and business applicability.
