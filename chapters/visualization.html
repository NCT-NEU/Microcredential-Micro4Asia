Data visualization for business insights#

MP Title#

Data visualization for business insights

  • Class contact: 15 hours

  • Self study: 45+ hours

MP Aim#

Provide learners with foundational knowledge and practical skills in data visualization, enabling them to design, construct, and present meaningful visual insights using Power BI, SQL, and Python to support evidence-based decision-making in business and related domains.

Target learners

  • Students from all disciplines seeking to improve data communication skills

  • Professionals involved in reporting, analytics, and management

  • Individuals preparing for data analyst or data-driven business roles

Assessment methods#

Assessment Type

Description

Purpose

Project-Based Assignment: Business Dashboard

learners work individually or in small groups to design and present a complete business dashboard using Power BI, based on a real or simulated dataset, demonstrating appropriate chart selection, layout design, and storytelling logic.

assesses integrated application of visualization principles, technical execution, and business insight communication.

Practical Assignment: Visualization Task

learners create specific visual components (charts, filters, dashboard sections) following given design requirements and business scenarios.

assesses practical ability to apply visualization tools and design principles.

Scenario-Based Quiz

multiple-choice and short-answer questions on visualization concepts, chart selection, and interpretation of visual outputs in business contexts.

tests conceptual understanding of data visualization principles.

Reflection & Peer Discussion

learners reflect on design choices and participate in peer discussion to evaluate clarity, effectiveness, and interpretability of dashboards.

assesses communication skills and critical evaluation of visual outputs.

Literature#

  • Wexler, S., Shaffer, J., & Cotgreave, A. (2017). The Big Book of Dashboards: Visualizing Your Data Using Real-World Business Scenarios. Wiley.

  • Kirk, A. (2019). Data Visualisation: A Handbook for Data Driven Design (2nd ed.). SAGE Publications.

  • Schwabish, J. A. (2021). Better Data Visualizations: A Guide for Scholars, Researchers, and Wonks. Columbia University Press.

  • Few, S. (2013). Information Dashboard Design: Displaying Data for At-a-Glance Monitoring (2nd ed.). Analytics Press.

Pre-requisites#

Basic knowledge of spreadsheets (Excel or Google Sheets) is recommended. No prior coding or visualization experience is required.

Additional notes#

This course emphasizes practical visualization skills and business storytelling. Visualization techniques are taught independently from data mining and modeling modules to ensure conceptual clarity and skill depth. Learners are expected to demonstrate both technical proficiency and the ability to communicate insights effectively to business stakeholders.
