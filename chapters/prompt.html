Prompt engineering#

MP Title#

Prompt engineering

  • Class contact: 20 hours

  • Independent study: 10 hours

MP Aim#

Develop practical competence in designing, refining, and applying effective prompts for generative AI systems, enabling learners to integrate AI tools into real-world workflows for communication, productivity, analysis, and strategic decision-making.

Foundations of prompt engineering and generative AI

Managing ambiguity and prompt clarity.

Zero-shot, few-shot and chain-of-thought prompting.
Role prompting and persona design.
Multi-step prompt chaining and task decomposition.

Prompt engineering for writing, summarization, analysis and planning tasks.
Designing prompts for business communication and reporting.

Prompting for task optimization and digital productivity.

Assessment methods and learners’ activities#

Assessment Type

Description

Purpose

Project-based assignment: prompt toolkit design

Learners design a mini prompt toolkit for a selected domain and demonstrate practical AI applications through case scenarios.

Assesses integrated ability to design, apply and evaluate prompt engineering strategies.

Scenario-based quiz

Multiple-choice and short-answer questions on prompt structures, AI limitations and applied techniques.

Tests conceptual understanding of prompt engineering principles.

Pre-requisites#

  • No formal AI programming experience required

  • Basic digital literacy and English comprehension recommended

Tools and platforms used#

  • ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Copilot

  • Office productivity software (Excel, Word, PowerPoint)

  • AI workflow tools such as Notion AI or task automation platforms

Literature#

  • Hunter, N. (2023). The art of prompt engineering with ChatGPT.

  • Prompt Engineering Guide. https://www.promptingguide.ai

  • OpenAI and Microsoft documentation on prompt engineering techniques.

Comments#

This micro-credential focuses on the practical application of prompt engineering for enhancing productivity, communication, and decision-making across diverse professional fields. The course emphasizes prompt clarity, workflow integration, critical evaluation, and ethical awareness, preparing learners to leverage generative AI systems effectively and responsibly in real-world contexts.
