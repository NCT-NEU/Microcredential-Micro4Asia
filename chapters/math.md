## Mathematical Foundations for Data Science
<hr>

### MP title

Mathematical Foundations for Data Science
- Class contact: 15 hours
- Independent study: 30 hours

### MP aim

Apply algebra and calculus concepts to support data representation, analytical modeling, and optimization in data science contexts.

### Learning outcomes 

| LO code | Bloom Level | Category | Learning outcome |
|---------|-------------|----------|------------------|
| LO.1 | Understand | Knowledge | Explain how data are structured and represented using vectors and matrices in data science contexts. |
| LO.2 | Understand | Knowledge | Describe key linear algebra concepts such as rank, linear independence, and matrix singularity in relation to data structure. |
| LO.3 | Understand | Knowledge | Explain the role of derivatives and function behavior in data modeling and optimization processes. |
| LO.4 | Apply | Skills | Apply vector and matrix operations to transform and prepare data for analysis. |
| LO.5 | Apply | Skills | Compute and interpret matrix rank and linear independence to assess data redundancy and structure. |
| LO.6 | Apply | Skills | Use derivatives to evaluate model sensitivity and support optimization tasks. |
| LO.7 | Analyze | Skills | Analyze the impact of algebraic and calculus-based transformations on data modeling performance. |

### Content

| Topic No. | Teaching topic | Content description | LO |
|-------|----------------|--------------------------------|---------|
| 1 | Data structure and representation | Representation of datasets as vectors and matrices.<br>Observation–feature matrix structure and interpretation of rows and columns.<br>Conversion of real-world business data into matrix form for analytical purposes. | LO.1 |
| 2 | Linear algebra foundations for data structure | Interpretation of rank as an indicator of effective data dimensionality.<br>Identification of linear independence and redundant variables.<br>Understanding of singular and non-singular matrices in relation to data quality and multicollinearity. | LO.2, LO.5 |
| 3 | Vector and matrix operations for data transformation | Application of matrix operations for data centering, scaling, and transformation.<br>Algebraic feature construction and data reshaping to enhance analytical readiness. | LO.4 |
| 4 | Functions and derivatives in data modeling | Conceptualization of data models as mathematical functions.<br>Use of derivatives to evaluate function behavior, sensitivity, and extrema.<br>Role of derivatives in loss functions for modeling contexts. | LO.3 |
| 5 | Derivatives and optimization techniques | Introduction to gradient concepts and their role in optimization.<br>Application of derivatives to minimize error functions.<br>Analysis of convergence behavior in basic optimization processes. | LO.6 |
| 6 | Impact of mathematical transformations on model performance | Evaluation of model performance before and after data transformation.<br>Effects of normalization and scaling on stability, sensitivity, and predictive accuracy. | LO.7 |


### Teaching and learning methods

| Teaching Method | Description | Related Learning Outcomes |
|----------------|-------------|---------------------------|
| Conceptual lectures & Visual demonstrations | builds foundational understanding of data representation, linear algebra concepts, and derivatives through structured explanation and visual illustration supported by Excel-based examples and demonstrations | LO.1, LO.2, LO.3 |
| Guided analytical practice | develops practical ability to perform data transformation, structural analysis, and apply derivatives using Excel formulas and functions | LO.4, LO.5, LO.6 |
| Case-Based Learning (Business & Economic Scenarios) | strengthens application of mathematical reasoning to real-world data modeling and business decision-making situations through Excel dataset | LO.3, LO.7 |
| Targeted Instructor Feedback & Reflection | enhances learning quality through feedback on analytical accuracy, reasoning clarity, and interpretation of results | LO.6, LO.7 |


### Assessment methods and learners' activities

| Assessment Type | Description | Purpose |
|----------------|-------------|----------|
| Practical Assignment: Mathematical Data Analysis | learners use Excel to apply vector, matrix, and derivative concepts to transform and analyze a provided business dataset, then interpret the mathematical implications for data structure and model performance. | assesses practical application of algebra and calculus in realistic, spreadsheet-based data contexts. |
| Scenario-Based Quiz | multiple-choice and short-answer questions on selecting appropriate mathematical concepts and analytical approaches for given economic and business scenarios. | tests conceptual understanding and decision-making in applying mathematical tools. |
| Reflection & Peer Discussion | learners reflect on their analytical choices and participate in structured peer discussion to explain and evaluate reasoning. | assesses interpretation, communication, and critical reflection skills. |

None

### Comments
Although this microcredential does not require any formal prerequisites, it is recommended that learners have previously completed a basic course in linear algebra and calculus to better support their understanding of the course content.
