## Applied machine learning in economics and business
<hr>

### MP Title

Applied machine learning in economics and business  
- Class contact: 15 hours  
- Independent study: 30 hours

---

### MP Aim

Develop practical competence in applying machine learning models to economic and business problems using Python, with emphasis on model construction, evaluation, interpretation, and decision support in real-world contexts.

---

### Learning outcomes 

| LO code | Bloom Level | Category | Learning outcome |
|---------|-------------|----------|------------------|
| LO.1 | Understand | Knowledge | Explain the role of machine learning models in solving economic and business decision-making problems. |
| LO.2 | Apply | Skills | Implement and train multiple machine learning models using Python libraries for predictive tasks. |
| LO.3 | Apply | Skills | Configure and compare machine learning models for different types of economic and business data. |
| LO.4 | Analyze | Skills | Evaluate model performance using appropriate validation metrics and diagnostic techniques. |
| LO.5 | Analyze | Skills | Interpret model results and translate outputs into actionable business or economic insights. |

---

### Content

| Topic No. | Teaching topic | Content description | LO |
|----------|----------------|--------------------|------|
| 1 | Foundations of applied machine learning in economics and business | Role of machine learning in finance, marketing, supply chain and risk management.<br>Overview of supervised learning models.<br>Python ML ecosystem including scikit-learn, numpy, pandas and matplotlib. | LO.1 |
| 2 | Regression models for economic forecasting | Linear regression, ridge regression and lasso regression. <br>Evaluation using RMSE and R² metrics. | LO.2, LO.3, LO.4 |
| 3 | Classification models for decision making | Logistic regression, k-nearest neighbors and support vector machines.<br>Applications in credit scoring and customer segmentation.<br>Accuracy, precision, recall and confusion matrix. | LO.2–LO.4 |
| 4 | Tree-based and ensemble models | Decision trees, random forest and gradient boosting models.<br>Model robustness and feature importance analysis.<br>Business use cases in churn prediction and fraud detection. | LO.2–LO.4 |
| 5 | Model evaluation and selection strategies | Cross-validation and model tuning concepts.<br>Bias–variance tradeoff explanation.<br>Selection criteria for business decision-making contexts. | LO.4 |
| 6 | Model interpretation and explainability | Interpretation of coefficients and tree structures.<br>Feature importance and explainability concepts.<br>Linking model predictions to strategic business decisions. | LO.5 |
| 7 | Integrated ML workflow case study | End-to-end application of multiple models on an economic or business dataset.<br>Comparison of model performance and decision implications. | LO.2–LO.5 |

### Teaching and learning methods

| Teaching Method | Description | Related learning outcomes |
|----------------|-------------|---------------------------|
| Conceptual lectures and model demonstrations | Introduces machine learning concepts and algorithms in applied economic and business contexts. | LO.1 |
| Guided hands-on coding practice | Develops practical ability to implement ML models using scikit-learn, pandas and numpy. | LO.2, LO.3 |
| Comparative modeling exercises | Enables learners to compare multiple models and identify optimal solutions. | LO.3, LO.4 |
| Case-based learning | Strengthens decision-oriented thinking using real-world economic and business datasets. | LO.4, LO.5 |
| Targeted instructor feedback and model review | Enhances interpretation and analytical reasoning skills. | LO.4, LO.5 |

---

### Assessment methods

| Assessment Type | Description | Purpose |
|----------------|-------------|----------|
| Project-based assignment: applied ML case study | Learners develop and compare multiple machine learning models for a real economic or business dataset and provide actionable recommendations. | Assesses integrated ability to apply, evaluate and interpret ML models. |
| Scenario-based quiz | Multiple-choice and short-answer questions on model selection, performance metrics and algorithm interpretation. | Tests conceptual understanding of applied ML principles. |

### Pre-requisites

- Mathematical foundations for data science  
- Python programming for data science  
- Data cleaning and preprocessing with Python  

---

### Libraries and tools used

- scikit-learn – machine learning algorithms  
- pandas – structured data handling  
- numpy – numerical computation  
- matplotlib – basic result visualization  
- joblib – model saving and deployment support

### Comments

This micro-credential emphasizes the practical deployment of machine learning techniques in economics and business applications. The course focuses on interpretable and applicable models that support informed decision-making, strategic planning, and performance optimization, while deliberately excluding deep learning to maintain clarity, relevance and business applicability.
