## Data visualization for business insights
<hr>

### Course title

Data Visualization for Business Insights

- Class contact: 15 hours  
- Self study: 45+ hours  

---

### Course aim

Provide learners with foundational knowledge and practical skills in data visualization, enabling them to design, construct, and present meaningful visual insights using Power BI, SQL, and Python to support evidence-based decision-making in business and related domains.

Target learners

- Students from all disciplines seeking to improve data communication skills  
- Professionals involved in reporting, analytics, and management  
- Individuals preparing for data analyst or data-driven business roles  

---

### Learning outcomes

| LO code | Bloom Level | Category | Learning outcome |

| LO.4 | Apply | Skills | Implement SQL queries for data extraction and preparation |
| LO.5 | Apply | Skills | Apply DAX functions to compute KPIs and business metrics |

### Course content

| Session | Teaching topic | Content description | Related LO |
|--------|----------------|--------------------|------------|
| 1 | Course introduction and data visualization workflow | Overview of data visualization in business contexts.<br>Role of visualization in the data-driven decision-making process.<br>Introduction to the end-to-end workflow: data → visualization → insight → communication. | LO.1 |
| 2 | Data types and chart selection principles | Classification of data types (categorical, numerical, temporal).<br>Principles for matching visualization types to analytical objectives.<br>Common visualization errors and best practices for chart selection. | LO.1, LO.2 |
| 3 | Power BI fundamentals | Importing datasets from Excel and external sources.<br>Basic data cleaning and transformation in Power BI.<br>Creating foundational visual elements: bar charts, line charts, and tables. | LO.3 |
| 4 | Interactive dashboards design | Designing dashboard layout and visual hierarchy.<br>Implementation of filters, slicers, and drill-down mechanisms.<br>Multi-view coordination and user-driven exploration techniques. | LO.3 |
| 5 | DAX for KPIs and forecasting | Concept of measures and calculated columns.<br>DAX functions for KPI computation and growth rate analysis.<br>Introduction to time intelligence and simple forecasting metrics. | LO.5 |
| 6 | SQL for data visualization workflows | Role of SQL in preparing data for visualization.<br>Core SQL commands: SELECT, WHERE, GROUP BY, JOIN.<br>Structuring datasets for Power BI integration. | LO.4 |
| 7 | SQL practice with customer dataset | Hands-on queries for customer segmentation and aggregation.<br>Applying joins and filters to generate reporting-ready datasets.<br>Preparing structured outputs for dashboard visualization. | LO.4 |
| 8 | Customer behavior analysis | Visualization of customer purchase patterns.<br>RFM segmentation concepts and dashboard representation.<br>Interpretation of customer value and engagement trends. | LO.2, LO.6 |
| 9 | Customer churn visualization | Visual representation of retention and attrition indicators.<br>Trend analysis of loyal versus high-risk customers.<br>Visual interpretation of churn risk patterns. | LO.2, LO.6 |
| 10 | Fraud and anomaly detection | Identification of abnormal data patterns using visual techniques.<br>Visual comparison of normal and suspicious behaviors.<br>Use of visualization to support fraud detection reasoning. | LO.2, LO.6 |
| 11 | Storytelling with data | Structuring narrative dashboards for business communication.<br>Techniques for presenting insights in logical sequences.<br>Designing persuasive visual stories for decision-makers. | LO.3, LO.6 |

### Teaching and learning methods

| Teaching Method | Description | Related LO |
|----------------|-------------|------------|
| Face-to-face lectures | delivery of foundational concepts and frameworks with real-time interaction | LO.1, LO.2 |
| Guided labs | hands-on practice using Power BI and SQL to build dashboards | LO.3, LO.4, LO.5 |
| Case-based learning | application of visualization principles to real business datasets | LO.2, LO.6 |
| Group discussion & presentation | peer-learning through reflection and insight sharing | LO.6 |
| Self-study | guided reading and independent practice | LO.1–LO.6 |

### Assessment methods

| Assessment Type | Description | Purpose |
|----------------|-------------|----------|
| Project-Based Assignment: Business Dashboard | learners work individually or in small groups to design and present a complete business dashboard using Power BI, based on a real or simulated dataset, demonstrating appropriate chart selection, layout design, and storytelling logic. | assesses integrated application of visualization principles, technical execution, and business insight communication. |
| Practical Assignment: Visualization Task | learners create specific visual components (charts, filters, dashboard sections) following given design requirements and business scenarios. | assesses practical ability to apply visualization tools and design principles. |
| Scenario-Based Quiz | multiple-choice and short-answer questions on visualization concepts, chart selection, and interpretation of visual outputs in business contexts. | tests conceptual understanding of data visualization principles. |
| Reflection & Peer Discussion | learners reflect on design choices and participate in peer discussion to evaluate clarity, effectiveness, and interpretability of dashboards. | assesses communication skills and critical evaluation of visual outputs. |

### Pre-requisites

Basic knowledge of spreadsheets (Excel or Google Sheets) is recommended. No prior coding or visualization experience is required.

### Additional notes

This course emphasizes practical visualization skills and business storytelling. Visualization techniques are taught independently from data mining and modeling modules to ensure conceptual clarity and skill depth. Learners are expected to demonstrate both technical proficiency and the ability to communicate insights effectively to business stakeholders.
