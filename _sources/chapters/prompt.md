## Prompt engineering
<hr>

### MP title

Prompt engineering  
- Class contact: 20 hours  
- Independent study: 10 hours  

---

### MP aim

Develop practical competence in designing, refining, and applying effective prompts for generative AI systems, enabling learners to integrate AI tools into real-world workflows for communication, productivity, analysis, and strategic decision-making.

---

### Learning outcomes

| LO code | Bloom Level | Category | Learning outcome |
|---------|-------------|----------|------------------|
| LO.1 | Understand | Knowledge | Explain the principles, structure, and limitations of prompt engineering in generative AI systems. |
| LO.2 | Understand | Knowledge | Describe core prompt patterns and interaction strategies for large language models. |
| LO.3 | Apply | Skills | Design effective prompts for diverse professional contexts including writing, analysis, and automation. |
| LO.4 | Apply | Skills | Implement multi-step prompts and role-based strategies to optimize AI outputs. |
| LO.5 | Analyze | Skills | Evaluate and refine prompts based on output quality and task objectives. |
| LO.6 | Analyze | Skills | Apply prompt engineering techniques to construct AI-supported workflows for practical problem-solving. |

---

### Content

| Topic No. | Teaching topic | Content description | LO |
|----------|----------------|--------------------|------|
| 1 | Foundations of prompt engineering and generative AI | Role of prompt engineering in generative AI systems.<br>Overview of large language models such as ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Copilot.<br>Core concepts of prompt structure and model interaction. | LO.1, LO.2 |
| 2 | Prompt design principles and anatomy | Elements of prompt construction including instructions, inputs, roles and constraints.<br>Comparison of effective and ineffective prompts.<br>Managing ambiguity and prompt clarity. | LO.1, LO.3 |
| 3 | Prompting techniques and strategies | Zero-shot, few-shot and chain-of-thought prompting.<br>Role prompting and persona design.<br>Multi-step prompt chaining and task decomposition. | LO.2, LO.4 |
| 4 | Applying prompts across professional tasks | Prompt engineering for writing, summarization, analysis and planning tasks.<br>Designing prompts for business communication and reporting. | LO.3, LO.4 |
| 5 | Integration of AI into workflows | AI-supported workflows using productivity tools such as Word, Excel, email and automation systems.<br>Prompting for task optimization and digital productivity. | LO.3, LO.6 |
| 6 | Prompt evaluation and ethical considerations | Evaluating prompt effectiveness and output reliability.<br>Understanding hallucination, bias and ethical risks.<br>Responsible use of generative AI in professional environments. | LO.5 |
| 7 | Integrated prompt engineering project | Design of an AI-powered solution for a selected domain.<br>Iterative prompt improvement and performance assessment.<br>Case study application and presentation. | LO.3–LO.6 |

### Teaching and learning methods

| Teaching Method | Description | Related learning outcomes |
|----------------|-------------|---------------------------|
| Conceptual lectures and live demonstrations | Introduces prompt engineering concepts and generative AI interaction through structured explanation and tool demonstrations. | LO.1, LO.2 |
| Guided hands-on practice | Develops skills in crafting and refining prompts through real-time experimentation. | LO.3, LO.4 |
| Case-based learning | Applies prompt engineering techniques to realistic professional scenarios. | LO.4, LO.6 |
| Collaborative discussion and reflection | Encourages critical thinking and evaluation of AI-generated outputs. | LO.5 |
| Targeted instructor feedback | Enhances prompt quality and strategic alignment through structured feedback. | LO.5, LO.6 |

---

### Assessment methods and learners' activities

| Assessment Type | Description | Purpose |
|----------------|-------------|----------|
| Project-based assignment: prompt toolkit design | Learners design a mini prompt toolkit for a selected domain and demonstrate practical AI applications through case scenarios. | Assesses integrated ability to design, apply and evaluate prompt engineering strategies. |
| Scenario-based quiz | Multiple-choice and short-answer questions on prompt structures, AI limitations and applied techniques. | Tests conceptual understanding of prompt engineering principles. |

### Pre-requisites

- No formal AI programming experience required

### Comments

This micro-credential focuses on the practical application of prompt engineering for enhancing productivity, communication, and decision-making across diverse professional fields. The course emphasizes prompt clarity, workflow integration, critical evaluation, and ethical awareness, preparing learners to leverage generative AI systems effectively and responsibly in real-world contexts.
